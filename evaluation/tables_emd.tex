\caption{Self-chosen (unconstrained) walking speed for the models capable of
free-speed generation, compared against the normative self-selected walking
speed reported by \citet{bohannon1997}. GaitNet's gait parameters are sampled
freely with the trained policy (model) held fixed. W2 is not used here (an
ensemble-vs-ensemble distance is not meaningful once each sample is free to
land at its own emergent speed); instead each generated cycle is matched to
its nearest available reference speed bin and scored by MAE (best
circular-shift alignment, \texttt{gait\_metrics.align\_and\_maes}) against (a)

per column, among the three genuinely free-choosing models, in \textbf{bold};
second-best \underline{underlined}. GaitEncoder has no force output. SIPP and
the Falisse-family predictive simulations solve with a hard forward-velocity

closest available substitute is a Falisse-settings (not SIPP contact model)
free-speed PredSim run, shown below the rule for reference only and excluded
from the bold/underline ranking -- its optimiser converged to a
cost-of-transport minimum far below the human self-selected range, so it is
